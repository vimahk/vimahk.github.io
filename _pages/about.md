Hi,

I started out as a software engineer and quickly got interested in low-level programming — C and Zig are the languages that scratch that itch. These days I work as a data engineer, building and tuning distributed data systems with Kotlin and Go. I spend my days designing pipelines, optimizing storage and queries, and wrestling with databases and data systems like Redis, ClickHouse, Scylla, and MongoDB.  
When I’m not staring at schemas or profiling I’m happiest outside — hiking, watching wildlife, or just hanging out with my friends.

I plan to write about my experiments, discoveries, and day-to-day experiences with different technologies. It will be a mix of notes, deep dives, and small findings that I find worth sharing.
